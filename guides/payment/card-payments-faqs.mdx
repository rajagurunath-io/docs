This page answers common questions about topping up IO Credits with a card (Fiat) through Stripe.

## General Payment Logic

- Compute is paid **only with IO Credits**, the platform's internal credits.
- You can **top up IO Credits with a credit or debit card** through Stripe.
- The minimum top-up is **\$10.00**.
- Card top-ups have a **5% payment provider fee**.

## FAQ

### Q. Can I pay for compute directly with a card?

No. Compute is paid only with IO Credits. Use your card to [top up your credits](/guides/payment/io-credits), then deploy.

### Q. How do I top up IO Credits with a card?

Click **Buy IO Credits**, enter the amount (at least **\$10.00**) and choose **USD (Credit/Debit Card)**. You're redirected to Stripe to complete the payment.

The Stripe payment page shows these line items:

- `IO Credits Cost`
- `Payment Provider Fee`

After a successful payment, the credits are added to your IO Credits balance.

### Q. How are fees displayed during payment?

When you pay through Stripe, you see:

- **IO Credits Cost**
- **Payment Provider Fee** (5%)
- **Total Amount**

### Q. Can I pay with my local currency?

Yes. If your local currency is supported by Stripe, you can pay with it.

Stripe automatically converts it to USD during checkout.

The final amount charged may vary slightly based on exchange rates and your bank's conversion fees.
