Pay-As-You-Go (PAYG) clusters have no fixed rental period. A cluster runs until you stop it or your credits run out, and you pay only for the time it runs, at the current price of the hardware.

## Summary

- You pay from your **IO credits**. [Top up your credits](/guides/payment/io-credits) before you deploy.
- The **first hour is charged when you deploy**. After that, you're charged every few minutes while the cluster runs.
- You always pay the **current price** for your hardware.
- If your credits run out, the cluster is **terminated**.
- Prepaid time is **not refunded** when you stop a cluster. If a cluster **fails**, the prepaid time is refunded.

## How billing works

### When you deploy

When you deploy a cluster, the first hour is charged from your credits right away. If you don't have enough credits for that hour, the cluster isn't deployed.

If you stop the cluster, this first hour is not refunded, even if you stop it a few minutes after it starts. If the cluster fails, it is refunded.

### While the cluster runs

After the first hour, your cluster is charged in small steps every few minutes, always slightly ahead of time, so it keeps running without interruption. Each charge uses the current price of the hardware, so if the price changes, the next charge reflects it.

All your PAYG clusters are paid from the same credit balance.

## When your credits run out

If your credits can't cover the next charge, the cluster is terminated. When several clusters share a low balance, the newest ones are terminated first.

To keep your clusters running, top up your credits when you get a low-balance warning.

<Warning>
  Termination removes the cluster. Back up any data you need before your credits run out.
</Warning>

## Notifications

We send email and in-app notifications so you can top up or stop clusters in time:

- **Low balance:** when your credits are getting low compared to how fast your clusters use them, with a final warning shortly before your clusters would be terminated.
- **Price change:** when the price of hardware your running clusters use changes.

## Stopping a cluster

Stop a cluster from its page in IO Cloud, or with the destroy endpoint for [VMs](/reference/vmaas/destroy-deployment) or [containers](/reference/caas/destroy-container). Billing stops when the cluster stops. Prepaid time that's left over is not refunded.

## Checking your burn rate

Your burn rate is how much your running PAYG clusters cost per hour, in total. To check it with the API, use [Get Burn Rate](/reference/io-cloud/get-burn-rate):

```bash
curl -X GET 'https://api.io.solutions/enterprise/v1/io-cloud/users/burn-rate' \
  -H "X-API-KEY: $IO_API_KEY"
```

```json
{
  "data": {
    "hourly_rate": 12.48,
    "runway_hours_estimate": 36.2,
    "cluster_count": 2
  }
}
```

- `hourly_rate`: what your running PAYG clusters cost per hour, in USD.
- `runway_hours_estimate`: about how many hours your credits will last at that rate.
- `cluster_count`: how many PAYG clusters you have running.

## Deploying PAYG clusters with the API

Set `billing_model` to `"payg"` in the deploy request:

- [Deploy a VM](/reference/vmaas/deploy-vms): `POST /enterprise/v1/io-cloud/vmaas/deploy`
- [Deploy a Container](/reference/caas/deploy-a-container): `POST /enterprise/v1/io-cloud/caas/deploy`

```bash
curl -X POST 'https://api.io.solutions/enterprise/v1/io-cloud/vmaas/deploy' \
  -H "X-API-KEY: $IO_API_KEY" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
    "resource_private_name": "my-payg-vm",
    "billing_model": "payg",
    "duration_hours": 1,
    "gpus_per_vm": 1,
    "hardware_id": 123,
    "location_ids": ["US"],
    "ssh_keys": {"my-key": "ssh-ed25519 AAAA..."}
  }'
```

`duration_hours` is still required in the request but has no effect on PAYG clusters.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I get a refund for unused time?">
    Not if you stop the cluster yourself. If the cluster fails, the prepaid time is refunded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my newest cluster terminated and not an older one?">
    All your clusters share one credit balance. When it runs low, the newest clusters are terminated first so the older ones can keep running.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pay for a PAYG cluster with IO Coin, USDC or a card?">
    Not directly. PAYG clusters are paid from credits. Use IO Coin, USDC or a card to [buy credits](/guides/payment/io-credits), then deploy.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
