Managed Services cover the cluster types that io.net provisions and configures for you, rather than deploying them yourself from IO Cloud. Our specialists size the cluster, set it up to your specifications, and hand it over ready to use.

The following cluster types are available as Managed Services:

- **Bare Metal:** Direct access to physical hardware with no virtualization layer.
- **Kubernetes:** A managed Kubernetes cluster for orchestrating GPU-accelerated container workloads.
- **Ray:** A managed Ray cluster for distributed Python, training, and inference workloads.
- **Confidential Compute:** Intel TDX-encrypted VMs that keep data, model weights, and computations encrypted in memory.

<Info>
  Self-service deployment for these cluster types is no longer available. Bare Metal on Demand was withdrawn on Wednesday, October 1, 2025. Virtual Machines and Containers remain self-service from IO Cloud.
</Info>

### To request a Managed Service:

1. Email our sales team at [business@io.net](mailto:business@io.net). Mention which cluster type you need and your preferred GPU type, and provide basic project details.

   <Note>
     Our specialists will reach out to understand your needs and assist with configuring the cluster to your specifications.
   </Note>
2. Once your cluster is provisioned, connect to it and deploy your workloads as usual.
